A stolen key isn't enough.

Create a smart account whose PIN lives in the contract. The PIN is checked under fully homomorphic encryption, and your spending rules are encrypted too: even whoever holds your key can't see them.

  • No 2FA server. Every PIN attempt is counted on-chain before its result is decrypted.
  • Your PIN never leaves this page. Only an encrypted fingerprint goes on-chain.
  • Hidden rules. Your limit is encrypted, and the real cap is a random point inside it.

      What happens when you send

      1. The transfer is opened on-chain with a fresh challenge.
      2. Your browser derives a fingerprint from the PIN (Argon2id) and encrypts fingerprint + challenge.
      3. The contract compares it with the stored encrypted fingerprint under FHE and counts the attempt.
      4. Zama's threshold network decrypts only the yes/no result, nothing else.
      5. On yes, the ETH moves; on no, nothing moves and the next attempt waits longer.
